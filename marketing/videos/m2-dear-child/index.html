Dear child,
To the child who reads a little slower,
you are not behind.
You are on your own path.
To the child who can't sit still,
moving is part of how you think.
To the child who needs the question twice,
asking again is how learning works.
You don't have to change to fit learning.
Learning should change to fit you.
With love,
the people building Learnigo
