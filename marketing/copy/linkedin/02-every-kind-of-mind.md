A child can know the answer and still lose, because the page went too fast or the clock ran out first.

So we built Learnigo around three numbers: every question read aloud, rounds of 10 questions, and 0 timers unless the child switches one on.

We designed it with dyslexia and ADHD in mind. What helps those children (audio, short rounds, no rush) turns out to help most children, which is why it is free for families and works in 14 languages.
