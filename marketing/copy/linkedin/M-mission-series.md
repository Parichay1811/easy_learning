## M1 · Born curious (16:9, 58 s)

**Company page (also add the video to the page's Featured section)**

```
Every child is born curious. Somewhere along the way, some of them stop asking.

Not because they can't learn. Because most learning was built for one kind of mind: read quickly, sit still, answer first time.

The child who reads slowly gets called behind. The child who can't sit still gets called difficult. Both of them stop putting their hand up.

We made a one-minute film about a simple idea. What if learning met children where they are? Words read aloud when reading is hard. More time when time is the problem. Moving when sitting still won't do.

Learning should be basic, like clean water. And easy, like play. For every child, in every home, in every language.

When did you last see a child stop asking questions, and what brought them back?

#Education #Neurodiversity
```

## M4 · Ten minutes (9:16, 24 s)

```
Ten minutes a day is small enough to keep and big enough to matter.

Not a battle at the kitchen table at the end of a long day. Ten minutes after dinner: a story, a game, a question answered out loud. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and the week takes care of itself.

We think learning should feel like part of family life, like brushing teeth or a bedtime story. Not more screen time. Better ten minutes.

Small, easy, every day. That's how big things grow.

What's the one small habit that actually stuck in your family?

#Parenting #Education
```
