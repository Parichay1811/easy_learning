## The post

| Rule | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| Hook in the first ~140 characters | Mobile cuts to "…see more" there. Line 1 is a **statement or a number**, never a question. An odd-precise number ("69 camera games", "10 questions") beats a round one. |
| 900–1,300 characters | ~150–220 words. Under 400 only for the established founder voice. Hard limit 3,000. |
| Short paragraphs | 1–2 sentences, blank line between ideas. No one-word paragraphs, no "Short. Punchy. Done." stacks. |
| One contrast, one triple | At most one "not X, it's Y" and one list of three, and the three are numbers or named things, never three adjectives. |
| No link in the body | Put it in the **first comment** and point to it ("Link in the first comment ↓"). |
| 0–2 hashtags, at the end | Niche beats popular. 5+ reads as spam. |
| End with a specific question | Anchored in the reader's experience ("What's the one homework task that ends in tears at your house?"). Never "Thoughts?" / "What do you think?". No "comment X to get Y", no "tag someone". |
| Product once, as the conclusion | The post is about the reader's problem. Learnigo appears as the answer, not the pitch. |
| Real stakes, no invented numbers | Every number in our posts is a product fact (14 languages, 69 camera games, 10-question rounds, 3 worlds). Founder posts need a **real, dated** moment from you; the drafts leave a marked slot for it. |

## Words we don't use

leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, delve, streamline, elevate, empower, unlock, harness,
foster, landscape, ecosystem, paradigm, journey, holistic, game-changer, deep dive,
"It's not just X, it's Y", "quietly", "let that sink in". Avoid 🚀 💡 🔑 and emoji on every line.

## Video on LinkedIn

- **Upload natively** (never a YouTube link: reach collapses).
- Vertical or 4:5 fills the phone feed; 1:1 is safe everywhere; 16:9 is fine for the company page.
- **Captions on**: most people watch muted. Our videos burn captions in; also upload the `.srt`.
- The first 3 seconds must hook visually. Ours open on a line of text, never a logo.
- 30–90 s is the sweet spot.

## Posting

- Tue / Wed / Thu, 07:30–09:00 in the audience's time zone (EU: 07:00–08:30 CET).
- Not Monday early, not Friday afternoon, not weekends. 2–3 posts a week, max one a day.
- 15 minutes before posting, leave 3–5 real comments on other people's posts.
- Reply to every comment in the first 60–90 minutes.
- Don't edit more than ~20% of the text in the first 3 hours.
- No engagement pods.
