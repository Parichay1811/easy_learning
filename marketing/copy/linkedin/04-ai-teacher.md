A child typed "Why do plants need sunlight?" and our AI teacher answered by drawing the leaf.

Sunlight on one side, water coming up the stem, carbon dioxide coming in, sugar and oxygen going out, then a short spoken explanation while each part lights up in turn.

That is the whole idea: a question becomes a small lesson on a board, not a wall of text.

Children ask by voice or by typing, and the answer is drawn and read aloud, so a child who struggles to read can still ask anything and follow the answer. They can tap any part of the drawing to hear more about it.

It works for maths, science, English and everyday questions, in the child's own language.

One decision we are proud of: the model runs on our own server, not through a third-party chatbot service.
