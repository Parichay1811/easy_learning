# Learnigo · The mission series ("Learning for every kind of mind")

A second set of videos, alongside the product videos in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md). These films don't
explain features. They tell a story people already feel and want to share:

> **Learning should be basic, like clean water, and easy, like play, for every child,
> in every home and every language.**

Learnigo appears only at the end, as a quiet signature, the way a charity or a values-led
brand signs a film. Nobody should feel sold to. They should feel understood, and then
remember who made them feel that way.

---

## 1. Why a mission series

- People share **beliefs**, not feature lists. A parent forwards "you are not behind" to
  another parent; nobody forwards "10-question rounds".
- LinkedIn rewards **stories with stakes** and real emotion. YouTube rewards films people
  watch to the end. Gentle, human films do both.
- It builds the brand Learnigo wants to be, a **part of family life and a better future**,
  before anyone needs the product. When they do, it is the name they trust.

## 2. Tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Speak to the child's and the parent's feelings | Show screens, prices, plans, feature lists |
| Slow and warm: pauses, room to breathe | Fast cuts, hype words, urgency |
| Hand-drawn line art on paper, like a parent's sketchbook | Glossy 3D, stock smiles |
| "Learning", "children", "every kind of mind" | "Users", "platform", "solution", "AI-powered" |
| Hopeful, never pitying | Sad-violin pity, deficit language ("suffers from") |
| Soft sign-off: logo, one line, the URL in small type | A call-to-action button, "Sign up now" |

**Respect the people in it.** We never say dyslexia or ADHD is something to fix. The point
is that learning should change to fit the child, not the other way round. Same claims rule
as PLAN.md: no medical words and no promises of outcomes.

## 3. Look and sound

- **Paper and pencil:** cream paper, ink-blue line drawings that draw themselves (SVG stroke
  animation), a few soft watercolour fills. Handwriting (Caveat) for short, big phrases;
  Fredoka for captions, so they stay readable for children with dyslexia.
- **The puppy** is the one recurring character. It listens, tilts its head, falls asleep on
  the sofa. It's a companion, not a mascot doing a pitch.
- **Music:** a slow piano-like bed (`assets/audio/bed-gentle.mp3`, generated in code by
  `tools/music.py gentle`).
- **Voice:** soft and slow (Kokoro `af_heart`, speed 0.9). A real parent or teacher reading
  these scripts would be even better.

## 4. The four films

| # | Film | Format | Length | Lead platform | The feeling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Born curious** | 16:9 | ~68 s | YouTube (brand film); LinkedIn | "Every child starts curious; we lose some of them; we don't have to." |
| M2 | **Dear child** | 4:5 | ~36 s | LinkedIn | A letter to children who learn differently. Shareable, quotable. |
| M3 | **Same wonder** | 9:16 | ~26 s | Shorts, Reels, LinkedIn | "Why?" in 14 languages: education for all, everywhere. |
| M4 | **Ten minutes** | 9:16 | ~31 s | Shorts, Reels, LinkedIn | Learning as part of the evening, not a battle. Lifestyle. |

### M1 · Born curious (script)

> Every child is born curious.
> They ask why the sky is blue. Why plants need the sun. Where the moon goes in the day.
> But somewhere along the way, some of them stop asking.
> Not because they can't learn, but because learning was built for one kind of mind.
> The child who reads slowly is called behind. The child who can't sit still is called difficult.
> What if learning met them where they are?
> Words read aloud, when reading is hard. More time, when time is the problem. Moving, when sitting still won't do.
> Learning should be basic, like clean water. And easy, like play.
> For every child. In every home. In every language.
> Because the future belongs to children who never stopped asking.
> *Learnigo. Learning for every kind of mind.*

### M2 · Dear child (script)

> To the child who reads a little slower: you are not behind. You are on your own path.
> To the child who can't sit still: moving is part of how you think.
> To the child who needs the question twice: asking again is how learning works.
> You don't have to change to fit learning. Learning should change to fit you.
> With love, the people building Learnigo.

### M3 · Same wonder (script)

> Why? ¿Por qué? Pourquoi? Warum? なぜ?
> Different words. The same wonder.
> Every child deserves to learn in the language they dream in.
> Fourteen languages today. Every child, tomorrow.
> *Learnigo. Learning for every kind of mind.*

### M4 · Ten minutes (script)

> Ten minutes, after dinner.
> No flashcards. No tears. Just a story, a game, a question answered out loud.
> Learning that feels like part of the day, not a battle at the end of it.
> Small, easy, every day. That's how big things grow.
> *Learnigo. Learning for every kind of mind.*

## 5. Publishing

- Lead the series with **M2 (Dear child)** on LinkedIn: it's the most shareable.
  Founder posts it with one real sentence about why.
- **M1 (Born curious)** goes on YouTube as the brand film and as the LinkedIn company page
  featured video. It also makes a good opening for talks and school presentations.
- **M3 and M4** go out as Shorts/Reels in alternate weeks with the product Shorts, so the
  channel isn't only features.
- Copy for every film is in `copy/linkedin/M*.md` and `copy/youtube/M*.md`, written as
  stories, with no feature lists and the link in the first comment.

| Week | LinkedIn | YouTube |

| 1 | M2 Dear child (company + founder) | M1 Born curious (brand film) |
| 2 | product video 04 (AI teacher) | M3 Same wonder (Short) |
| 3 | M1 Born curious (company page featured) | product 05 (Short) |
| 4 | M4 Ten minutes | M4 Ten minutes (Short) |

## 6. How to tell if it worked

Mission films are judged on **sharing and watch time**, not clicks:
reposts and saves on LinkedIn, comments where people tell their own story, average view
duration on YouTube, and new subscribers. Expect fewer sign-ups per view than the
product videos, and more people who already trust the name when they do arrive.
