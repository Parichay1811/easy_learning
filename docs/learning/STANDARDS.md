## Developmental order: changes made in content v5

Where a skill was placed later than the curricula teach it, the skill (and its items) moved, so the ladder climbs in the order children develop:

- **english.syllables**: level 3 → 1. Strong. Syllable awareness is one of the earliest phonological skills: Development Matters lists 'count or clap syllables in a word' for 3–4-year-olds; CCSS RF.K.2b is kindergarten; PLC Reading LO4 (Stage 1) lists syllables before rhyme, onset-rime and phonemes. At level 3 it comes after rhyme (2) and first/last sounds (2), which reverses the usual progression syllable → rhyme/onset-rime → phoneme.
- **english.rhyme**: level 2 → 1. Development Matters (3–4-year-olds): 'spot and suggest rhymes'; CCSS RF.K.2a (kindergarten); PLC Reading LO4 Stage 1 (junior/senior infants).
- **english.first-last-sounds**: level 2 → 1. Development Matters (3–4-year-olds): 'recognise words with the same initial sound'; EYFS ELG Writing (end of Reception) spells words by identifying their sounds; CCSS RF.K.2d (kindergarten); PLC Reading LO4 Stage 1.
- **english.word-families**: level 4 → 2. Strong. Phoneme substitution to make new words is CCSS RF.K.2e (kindergarten); decoding similarly spelled words is RF.K.3d/RF.1.3b; England Year 1 phonics; PLC Reading LO4 (manipulate onset-rime and phonemes) Stages 1–2. Its only prerequisite (spelling-short-words) is level 1.
- **english.answering-aloud**: level 4 → 1. Strong. PLC Oral Language LO7 (ask and answer open and closed questions) is Stage 1–2; EYFS ELG Listening, Attention and Understanding (end of Reception); CCSS SL.K.3. Answering a spoken question is an early oral-language skill and the prerequisite for speaking-opinions.
- **english.following-instructions**: level 3 → 1. EYFS ELG Self-Regulation (end of Reception): 'follow instructions involving several ideas or actions'; PLC Oral Language LO6 (give and follow instructions) Stages 1–2; CEFR Pre-A1/A1 understanding instructions. Multi-step instructions could stay at level 2.
- **english.sequencing**: level 4 → 2. PLC Reading LO9 'recall, discuss and sequence significant details' and Oral Language LO9 retelling with sequencing are Stage 1–2; EYFS ELG Comprehension (retelling); CCSS RL.K.2/RL.1.2; England Year 1 maths 'sequence events in chronological order'.
- **english.describing**: level 4 → 2. PLC Oral Language LO12 Description (Stages 1–2); CCSS SL.K.4/SL.1.4; EYFS ELG Speaking. Spoken description is an infant-class skill; a written descriptive sentence is Year 1 (England) – level 2 covers both.
- **english.capitals-punctuation**: level 3 → 2. England Year 1 statutory: 'beginning to punctuate sentences using a capital letter and a full stop'; Development Matters (Reception): write short sentences 'using a capital letter and full stop'; CCSS L.K.2a/L.K.2b, L.1.2b; PLC Writing LO3 Stage 1.
- **english.word-endings**: level 3 → 2. England Year 1 statutory spelling: adding –s/–es and –ing, –ed, –er, –est where the root does not change; CCSS RF.1.3f and L.1.4c (grade 1); PLC Stage 2. Year 2 extends suffixes (–ment, –ness, –ful, –less, –ly); those could stay at level 3 if split out.
- **english.sight-words**: level 3 → 2. CCSS RF.K.3c (high-frequency words by sight) and RF.K.3d (distinguish similarly spelled words) are kindergarten; England Year 1 common exception words; PLC Reading LO5 Stage 1.
- **english.word-groups**: level 3 → 2. PLC Oral Language LO8 Categorisation is Stage 1–2; CCSS L.K.5a sorts objects into categories (K) and L.1.5a sorts words into categories (grade 1).
- **english.composition**: level 5 → 3. England Year 1 statutory: 'sequencing sentences to form short narratives'; EYFS ELG Writing: 'write simple phrases and sentences'; CCSS W.1.2/W.1.3 (grade 1: two or more sequenced events); PLC Writing LO6/LO7 Stages 1–2. Evidence points to level 2; level 3 allows for the writing load in dyslexia.
- **english.speaking-opinions**: level 5 → 3. PLC Oral Language LO7 'express personal needs, opinions and preferences' (Stages 1–2) and LO11 'supply, explain and justify points of information' (Stages 1–2); England Spoken language (Years 1–6) 'articulate and justify answers, arguments and opinions'; EYFS ELG Speaking 'offer explanations for why things might happen'.
- **english.reading-comprehension**: level 4 → 3. Mild. Answering questions about key details in a text read independently is CCSS RL.1.1/RI.1.1 (grade 1); England Years 1–2; PLC Reading LO9 Stages 1–2.
- **english.spelling-correction**: level 5 → 4. Mild. England Year 2: 'proof-reading to check for errors in spelling, grammar and punctuation'; CCSS L.2.2e (grade 2) and L.3.2g; PLC Writing LO4 Stage 2 begins checking and correcting spelling.
- **english.spelling-aloud**: level 4 → 2. Mild. England Year 1 statutory: name the letters of the alphabet in order and use letter names to distinguish spellings; CCSS RF.K.1d (name all letters). If it is kept later on purpose because oral spelling loads phonological working memory (a dyslexia difficulty), record that as a deliberate choice.
- **maths.fractions**: level 5 → 3. Strong. Halves and quarters are England Year 1 and thirds Year 2; CCSS 1.G.A.3 (halves, fourths) and 2.G.A.3 (halves, thirds, fourths); PMC 2023 Number – Fractions Stage 2 (1st–2nd class) 'recognise and name fractions'. Halves/quarters of shapes could be level 2. Its prerequisite should be sharing-equally (level 1) rather than multiplication.
- **maths.time**: level 3 → 2. England Year 1 (o'clock, half past) and Year 2 (quarter past/to, five minutes); CCSS 1.MD.B.3 (hours and half-hours) and 2.MD.C.7 (five minutes); PMC 2023 Measures – Time Stage 2. Within the skill keep the order o'clock → half past → quarter past/to (PMC Time continuum: hours and half hours before half and quarter hours).
- **maths.comparing-groups**: level 2 → 1. EYFS ELG Numerical Patterns (end of Reception): compare quantities up to 10 – greater than, less than or the same; CCSS K.CC.C.6 'by using matching and counting strategies' (kindergarten); PMC Stage 1. Its only prerequisite is counting (level 0).
- **maths.number-sentences**: level 3 → 2. England Year 1 statutory: 'read, write and interpret mathematical statements involving +, – and ='; CCSS K.OA.A.1/K.OA.A.3 (record with equations) and 1.OA.D.7; PMC Algebra – Expressions and equations Stage 2. It currently sits above missing-number (2) and choosing-operation (2), which both assume the learner can read a number sentence.
- **maths.data**: level 4 → 2. England Year 2 Statistics: 'interpret and construct simple pictograms'; CCSS 1.MD.C.4 (grade 1) and 2.MD.D.10 picture graphs (grade 2); PMC Data Stages 1–2.
- **maths.money**: level 4 → 3. Mild. England Year 2: simple money problems 'including giving change'; CCSS 2.MD.C.8 (grade 2); PMC Measures – Money Stage 2.
- **maths.word-problems**: level 3 → 2. Mild. One-step addition/subtraction word problems are CCSS K.OA.A.2 (within 10) and 1.OA.A.1 (within 20) and England Year 1. Reading load can be removed with audio.
- **maths.estimation**: level 4 → 3. Mild. PMC 2023 Place value Stage 2: 'use estimation to quickly determine number values'; England Year 2 'identify, represent and estimate numbers'.
- **maths.fractions-decimals-percentages**: level 6 → 5. Mild. England Year 5 (per cent, decimal equivalents) and Year 6 (FDP equivalences, percentages of amounts); PMC 2023 Stage 4 (5th–6th class) 'explore the relationships between fractions, decimals and percentages'. CCSS puts percent at grade 6 (6.RP.A.3c), so level 6 is defensible for US alignment.
- **maths.powers**: level 8 → 7. Mild. Square and cube numbers are England Year 5; whole-number exponents are CCSS 6.EE.A.1 (grade 6); square/cube roots are 8.EE.A.2 (grade 8). The prerequisite linear-equations is not needed for squares, cubes and powers of ten; multiplication is enough.
- **science.living-things**: level 1 → 2. STE 2025 places 'understand and record the difference between living and non-living things' at Stage 2 (1st–2nd class); England places it in Year 2 (living, dead, never alive).
- **science.light-sound**: level 1 → 2. NGSS 1-PS4-1 (sound and vibration) and 1-PS4-2 (seeing needs light) are grade 1; STE 2025 places sound at Stage 2 and light at Stage 3; England Year 3 (Light) and Year 4 (Sound). The 1999 Irish curriculum had light and sound in infants, so level 2 is a compromise.
- **science.weather-seasons**: level 2 → 1. Mild. EYFS ELG The Natural World (the seasons, end of Reception); England Year 1 Seasonal changes; NGSS K-ESS2-1 (kindergarten); 1999 Irish infants (seasonal change).
- **science.cause-effect**: level 3 → 2. Mild. STE 2025 Materials Stage 1: 'explore, observe and discuss the cause and effect of changes to everyday materials'; EYFS ELG Speaking: 'offer explanations for why things might happen'.
- **science.observation**: level 3 → 2. Mild. EYFS ELG The Natural World (making observations); England KS1 Working scientifically (observing closely); STE 2025 Living things Stages 1–2 (use senses to make scientific observations).

Kept on purpose: spelling aloud at level 4 (oral spelling loads working memory, which is hard for dyslexic learners); fractions, decimals and percentages at level 6 (US grade 6); powers at level 8 (the maths stretch levels).
